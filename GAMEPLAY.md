## Core Gameplay

BrogueMCP is built on the foundation of Brogue, a classic roguelike known for its elegant design and procedurally generated dungeons. The core gameplay remains true to the original:

- **Permadeath**: When your character dies, the game is over. No saves, no continues.
- **Procedural Generation**: Every dungeon is uniquely generated, ensuring no two playthroughs are alike.
- **Turn-based Movement**: The game advances one turn at a time as you move or act.
- **Strategic Combat**: Position, resources, and careful decision-making matter more than twitch reflexes.

The objective is to descend to the 26th level of the dungeon, retrieve the Amulet of Yendor, and return to the surface. For an extra challenge, you can continue deeper to collect lumenstones.

## The Dungeon Master AI

What sets BrogueMCP apart is the AI-powered Dungeon Master that enhances your adventure with rich storytelling:

### Narrative Events

The DM generates atmospheric descriptions for key moments:

- **Monster Encounters**: First-time meetings with creatures
- **Monster Defeats**: Especially for rare or powerful foes
- **Item Discoveries**: Finding unique or important items
- **Level Transitions**: Entering new dungeon depths
- **Player Death**: A dramatic epitaph for your fallen adventurer

### Memory System

The DM remembers your journey and references past events:

- **Recurring Enemies**: The DM recalls previous encounters with the same monster types
- **Progressive Narrative**: The storytelling evolves based on your choices and experiences
- **Persistent Character Arc**: Your adventure becomes a coherent story rather than disconnected events

### Customizable Narrator

Press `N` during gameplay to customize your storytelling experience:

- **Personality Presets**: Choose styles like Gandalf (wise, measured), Aragorn (brave, direct), or Galadriel (mysterious, ethereal)
- **Tone Adjustments**: Modify attributes like wisdom level, verbosity, and formality
- **Thematic Elements**: Control nature references, metaphor use, and other stylistic elements

## Controls

### Basic Movement

- **Arrow Keys** or **Numpad** or **Vi Keys (hjklyubn)**: Move in 8 directions
- **Rest (.)**: Wait one turn
- **Run (Shift + direction)**: Move continuously in a direction until interrupted
- **Search (s)**: Search adjacent tiles for secret doors or traps
- **Autoexplore (o)**: Automatically explore the current level

### Combat and Items

- **Melee Attack**: Move into an enemy
- **Throw (t)**: Throw an item
- **Apply (a)**: Use a staff, charm, or wand
- **Equip (e)**: Equip a weapon or armor
- **Remove (r)**: Remove an equipped item
- **Drop (d)**: Drop an item
- **Pickup (,)**: Pick up an item

### Special Commands

- **Inventory (i)**: View inventory
- **Examine (x)**: Look around and examine tiles
- **Descend (<)**: Go down stairs
- **Ascend (>)**: Go up stairs
- **Customize Narrator (N)**: Open the narrator customization interface
- **Help (?)**: View help screen
- **Save (S)**: Save and exit the game

## Character Development

Unlike many RPGs, Brogue doesn't have character classes or experience levels. Your development comes through:

### Equipment

- Finding and equipping better weapons and armor
- Enchanting your gear to enhance its capabilities
- Discovering and using powerful artifacts

### Magic Items

- **Scrolls**: One-time magical effects (identify, enchantment, protection)
- **Potions**: Consumable effects (healing, strength, invisibility)
- **Staves**: Rechargeable magical tools with limited charges
- **Wands**: Similar to staves but with a fixed number of charges
- **Charms**: Reusable items that recharge over time
- **Rings**: Passive benefits while equipped

### Allies

- Free captive creatures to gain allies
- Empower allies with wands of empowerment to let them gain new abilities
- Strategically use allies to overcome challenging situations

## Combat and Tactics

Combat in BrogueMCP is tactical and position-based:

### Weapon Types

- **Swords**: Balanced damage and speed
- **Axes**: Can hit multiple enemies in one swing
- **Maces**: Higher damage but slower
- **Spears**: Can attack enemies from two tiles away
- **Daggers**: Fast attacks but lower damage
- **Rapiers**: Triple damage when lunging (moving into combat from two spaces away)

### Armor Types

- **Leather**: Light protection with minimal stealth penalties
- **Scale**: Medium protection with moderate penalties
- **Mail**: Heavy protection with significant stealth penalties
- **Plate**: Maximum protection but heavy stealth and speed penalties

### Runic Enhancements

Weapons and armor can have special runic properties:

- **Weapon Runics**: Slaying, vampirism, quietus, paralysis, multiplicity, and more
- **Armor Runics**: Reflection, immunity, absorption, reprisal, and more

### Terrain Tactics

- Use doorways as choke points
- Take advantage of water to escape fire or create distance
- Utilize chasms and bridges for strategic positioning
- Create defensive positions with obstruction staves

## Items and Equipment

### Identification

- Items start unidentified
- Use scrolls of identify or learn through experimentation
- Auto-identification occurs in certain situations

### Enchantment

- Scrolls of enchantment are the most valuable resource
- Use them to enhance your weapons, armor, staves, or charms
- Strategic enchantment decisions are crucial for success

### Key Items

- **The Amulet of Yendor**: The primary objective, found on depth 26
- **Lumenstones**: Found below depth 26, each worth 500 gold
- **Keys**: Open special vaults containing valuable equipment

## Game Variants

BrogueMCP includes multiple game variants:

### Standard Brogue

- The classic experience with 26 dungeon levels plus optional deeper levels
- Balanced difficulty curve
- Complete feature set

### Rapid Brogue

- Faster-paced variant where powerful items appear earlier
- Condensed dungeon of 14 levels
- Perfect for shorter play sessions

## Tips and Strategies

### General Advice

- Don't fight everything - sometimes running is the best option
- Food is limited - don't dawdle on levels too long
- Identify potions by dropping them in water to see if they cause effects
- Use fire to burn away foliage for better visibility
- Save enchantment scrolls for items with good runics

### Combat Tips

- Let monsters come to you in corridors to fight them one-on-one
- Use your environment - traps, chasms, and lava can all help defeat enemies
- Allies are powerful but require protection
- Not all monsters need to be defeated - sometimes avoidance is best

### Resource Management

- Manage your food carefully
- Use staves and charms strategically - recharging takes time
- Balance exploration with the need to conserve resources
- Be mindful of strength requirements for equipment

### Dungeon Master Integration

- Pay attention to the DM's descriptions for subtle hints
- The narrative can provide atmospheric context that enhances your decisions
- Customize the narrator to match your preferred storytelling style
- The DM remembers your journey - your story builds as you progress

---

Remember that learning to play Brogue well takes time. Death is frequent, especially for new players, but each defeat teaches valuable lessons. The DM will be there to narrate your triumphs and tragedies alike, making even your defeats memorable!
